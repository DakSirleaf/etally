Inhibitor vs Inducer Logic
⊗ Inhibitor
Blocks the enzyme — substrate levels rise. Risk: toxicity. Traffic jam on the metabolic highway.
↑ Inducer
Revs up the enzyme — substrate levels fall. Risk: treatment failure. Highway widens, cars clear faster.
